Delete your data

Arrow Tap · by UniqueLab

You can ask us to delete the data that Arrow Tap (package com.uniquelab.arrowtap) stores about you. You do not need an account.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Arrow Tap and note the display name you use on the leaderboard (Settings › your profile).
  2. Send an email to support.uniquelab@gmail.com with the subject "Arrow Tap data deletion" and include your display name.
  3. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entry: display name, avatar, frame, country code and scores.
  • The random anonymous user ID linked to that entry.

What is kept

  • Nothing about you is kept after deletion, except our email exchange, which we keep for up to 90 days as a record of the request.
  • Game progress saved on your phone (levels, gold, diamonds, settings) is not sent to us. You can delete it yourself at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Advertising data handled by Google AdMob is managed by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

More details: Privacy Policy.
