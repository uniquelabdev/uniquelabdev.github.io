Delete your data

Arrow Tap · by UniqueLab · Last updated: November 23, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data that Arrow Tap (package com.uniquelab.arrowtap) stores about you. You do not need an account.

Delete it in the app

Open Arrow Tap › Leaderboard and tap the Leave button at the top right. Your leaderboard entry and the random player ID are removed right away (an internet connection is needed).

Or request deletion by email

  1. Open Arrow Tap › Leaderboard and note your display name (shown in your own row at the bottom).
  2. Send an email to support.uniquelab@gmail.com with the subject "Arrow Tap data deletion" and include your display name.
  3. We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entry: display name, country code and completed-level counts.
  • The random player ID linked to that entry.

What is kept

  • Nothing about you is kept after deletion, except our email exchange, which we keep for up to 90 days as a record of the request.
  • Game progress saved on your phone (levels, picture pieces, Math Puzzle progress, settings) is not sent to us. You can delete it yourself at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Advertising data is handled by our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads) under the Unity Privacy Policy, where you can also make requests to them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
