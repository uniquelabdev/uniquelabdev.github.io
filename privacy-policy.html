Privacy Policy

Arrow Tap · Effective date: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Tap (package com.uniquelab.arrowtap), developed by MD Abdul Halim, an independent developer from Bangladesh ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, gold, diamonds, lives, daily reward streak, sound and notification settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Leaderboard (Firebase)

To show the online leaderboard, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a random user ID. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number or password.
  • Firebase Realtime Database stores your leaderboard entry: the random user ID, your display name, chosen avatar and frame, country code (taken from your device's region setting) and level/score progress.

Your display name, avatar, country flag and score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real full name or personal information as your display name.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local reminders (for example, about your daily reward). These are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to us for this.

5. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob – Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase – Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Play Services

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the Google services listed above only to provide the leaderboard and ads. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the game is offered. To delete your leaderboard entry, email us at the address below, including your display name; we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

8. Children

Arrow Tap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
