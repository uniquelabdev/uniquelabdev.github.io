Privacy Policy

Arrow Tap · Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Tap (package com.uniquelab.arrowtap), developed by MD Abdul Halim, an independent developer under the name UniqueLab ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, hearts, hints, stars, picture pieces and your Picture Collection, Math Puzzle and Daily Challenge progress, your streak, sound, music and reminder settings) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Leaderboard

The online leaderboard is optional. Nothing is sent to our leaderboard server unless you tap Join on the Leaderboard screen. If you join, the app sends the following to our leaderboard server:

  • A random player ID created by the server. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account, and no sign-in or password is used.
  • Your display name, your country code (taken from your device's region setting, or the country you choose; you can also hide it and show a globe instead) and the number of levels you completed each day over the last 30 days.

Your display name, country flag and level count are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real full name or other personal information as your display name.

3. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads)

The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service by Unity Technologies and its affiliate ironSource. The ads come from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads. These services may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (derived from the IP address), device and app information, app diagnostics and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

In regions where it is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy Policy › Ad privacy choices.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local reminders (for example, about the Daily Challenge and your streak). They are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to us for this. You can turn them off in the app under Settings › Daily reminders or in your device settings.

5. Update check

When the Home screen opens, the app reads a small file from our website (uniquelabapps.com) to see whether a newer version is available. No personal information is sent with this request.

6. Sharing pictures

When you share a completed picture, it is passed to the app you choose through your phone's Share menu. Nothing is sent to us.

7. Third-party services

  • Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads – Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (advertising ID) – Google Privacy Policy

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information for money. Leaderboard data is used only to show the leaderboard. Advertising data is collected and processed by our ad partners listed above to provide, measure and protect ads, as described in their own privacy policies. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Where data is stored and security

Leaderboard data is stored on secure cloud servers used by us to run the leaderboard, which may be located in other countries. Advertising data is processed by our ad partners, which may handle it in other countries under their own safeguards. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the leaderboard server only accepts changes to an entry from the app that created it (each player ID is protected by a signed key).

10. Legal basis and your rights

We process leaderboard data to provide the online leaderboard you choose to use, and advertising data based on your consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), otherwise based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time (in the app under Settings › Privacy Policy › Ad privacy choices). To use these rights, email us at the address below. For advertising data, you can also contact Unity through its privacy policy. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries are kept while the game is offered. You can leave the leaderboard at any time: open the Leaderboard screen and tap the Leave button at the top right. This removes your entry and the random player ID from our server. You can also email us at the address below, including your display name; we will delete it within 30 days. See Delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

12. Children

Arrow Tap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
